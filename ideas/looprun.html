Features

Smart routing,
right on your wrist.

LoopRun combines real-time map data with intelligent route optimization to give you the perfect run — every time.

🎯

Precise Distance Matching

Tell us you want 7km, and you get 7km. Our algorithm finds paths that match your target within 100 meters — no overshooting, no cutting short.

🔄

Loop or Linear — Your Choice

End where you started for a classic loop run, or route to a specific destination. Heading to the office? We'll get you there at the right distance.

🗺️

Scenic Route Priority

We prioritize parks, waterfronts, and pedestrian-friendly streets. Your route avoids highways and dead-ends automatically.

⌚

Glance & Go

Set your distance on Apple Watch, get your route instantly. No phone needed. Turn-by-turn haptic guidance keeps you on track.

🧭

Live Recalculation

Took a detour or wandered off? LoopRun recalculates the remaining path in real time to keep your total distance on target.

📊

Auto-Sync to Health

Every run syncs seamlessly with Apple Health and Activity rings. Track pace, heart rate, elevation, and calories — all in one place.

How It Works

Three taps to your perfect run.

No planning, no map-staring, no "is this route actually 5K?" Just set, go, and let your watch handle the rest.

Set Your Distance

Rotate the Digital Crown or tap to choose anywhere from 1 to 20 kilometers. Pick loop-back or destination mode.

Get Your Route

LoopRun calculates the optimal path in under 3 seconds. See the map, distance, estimated time, and elevation profile.

Run & Arrive

Follow haptic turn-by-turn guidance. Arrive at exactly the right spot, at exactly the right distance. Done.

Route Engine

Every route,
optimized.

LoopRun's routing engine doesn't just find a path — it finds the right path. Here's what goes into every calculation:

  • ✓ Pedestrian-safe streets only — no highways, no sketchy crossings
  • ✓ Scenic priority — parks, waterfronts, and greenways preferred
  • ✓ Elevation awareness — optional flat routes for easy days
  • ✓ Minimized repetition — no running the same block twice
  • ✓ Real-time traffic and construction avoidance
  • ✓ Privacy-first — all routing done on-device where possible
