Features

Everything a
stranger-to-stranger
marketplace needs.

💬

Order-thread Chat

Buyer and seller talk inside the order itself. Every promise and price agreement is logged — and visible to support if things go sideways. No off-platform deals.

🧮

True Landed Price

Listing prices are in local currency. Before you check out, we show the real total: item + international shipping + estimated duties. No surprise bills at the door.

🚚

Live Tracking Built In

Sellers attach a tracking number; we auto-import status from 1,200+ carriers worldwide. "Delivered" triggers the confirmation countdown automatically.

⏱️

Auto-release Protection

If the buyer forgets to confirm, funds release automatically after a safe window (tracking delivered + 3 days). Sellers are never left waiting indefinitely.

⚖️

Dispute Freeze

Item not as described? Open a dispute before the window closes and the money freezes instantly. Both sides upload evidence; our team settles it fairly.

🌐

Get Paid Anywhere

Sellers receive payouts in their own currency and local bank — cards, bank transfer, or e-wallets. Powered by marketplace-grade payment rails, not our own wallet.
