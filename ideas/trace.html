Log Types

One app.
Every kind of log.

Stop juggling five different apps for five different things. Trace gives you dedicated log types for each part of your life — and you can create your own.

💰

Expense Log

Track spending with amount, category, merchant, and payment method. Snap a photo of the receipt. See where your money goes.

📍

Place Visit Log

Log where you've been — restaurants, parks, shops. Auto-fill location, add photos, rate your experience, and build a personal map.

👥

Meeting Log

Record attendees, agenda, decisions, and action items. Attach photos of whiteboards or slides. Never lose meeting context again.

✨

Custom Logs

Create your own log type with custom fields. Track workouts, books, wine, plants — anything. Define your fields once, log forever.

Dual View Mode

Read it your way.

Some days you want data at a glance. Other days you want to relive the moment. Toggle between text and photo views anytime — same entries, different lens.

How It Works

Three steps. That's it.

No setup, no categories to define upfront. Just open, log, and go.

Pick a Log Type

Choose from Expense, Place, Meeting, or your custom types. Or just start typing — Trace will suggest a type.

Fill & Attach

Add details, snap or upload photos, jot a note. Auto-fill handles location, time, weather, and more.

Browse & Drill In

Switch between text and photo views. Filter by type, date, or tag. Tap any entry to see the full story.
